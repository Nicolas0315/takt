## Finding Contract Additional Procedure

- Apply every section of Contract Lifecycle Knowledge in the same review as the architecture perspective
- For each changed requirement or contract, trace the public entry point, producer, validator, consumer, and corresponding tests
- For each changed resource, trace owner and transfer, last consumer, and release or persistence across success, failure, interruption, and retry paths
- Do not fill evidence gaps by inference; mark unverified items explicitly in the output contract evidence tables
